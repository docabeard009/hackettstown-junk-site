The Pricing Model

Simple, Volume-Based Pricing

Our pricing is based on one thing: how much space your items take up in our truck. The more space, the higher the price. The less space, the lower the price. It's that straightforward.

We don't charge by the hour. We don't charge by item count. And we don't add surprise fees at the end. Our truck team arrives, looks at your junk, and gives you one firm price — all labor, disposal, recycling, and cleanup included.

The best part? There's absolutely no obligation. If the price doesn't work for you, just say so and we'll be on our way. No pressure, no hard feelings.

What We Take — and What We Don't

  • Mattresses & box springs

  • Yard waste, brush & landscaping debris
  • Hot tubs, sheds & outdoor structures
  • General household clutter & boxes
  • Estate & full-home cleanouts

✕ What We Cannot Take

For safety and NJ environmental compliance, we cannot haul these hazardous materials. Not sure about your item? Just call and we'll tell you right away.

  • Liquid / Wet Paint
  • Chemical Solvents
  • Asbestos
  • Biohazard / Medical Waste
  • Propane Tanks
  • Motor Oil & Gasoline
  • Pesticides
  • Car Batteries
  • Radioactive Materials
  • Explosives

Dried latex paint in open cans is usually accepted. Call us to confirm.
